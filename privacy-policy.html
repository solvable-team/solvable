Privacy Policy

Solvable Team ("we," "us," or "our") made this policy to explain how Solvable: Mental Math (the "Application") handles information while the app is in closed testing on Google Play.

1. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE

The Application does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, analytics, or usage data.

2. INFORMATION THAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

All application data, including practice history, game statistics, settings, and problem-solving performance, is stored strictly on your local device.

  • We do not transmit, back up, or retain access to this information.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this local data.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The Application does not use third-party analytics, ad networks, or external tracking SDKs. No data is transmitted off your device to us or any external parties.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Solvable: Mental Math does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
